## HVAC Excellence in Riverside

Riverside's reputation as a growing regional hub is matched by the demands placed on its residents' home comfort systems. Our region's summer heat and humidity are among the most intense around, making a reliable, properly maintained HVAC system essential for daily comfort and health.

Our technicians work throughout all of Riverside's diverse neighborhoods. We're experienced with the full range of residential and light commercial systems found across the city — from packaged rooftop units common in older commercial buildings to modern split systems in new residential construction.

### Riverside Climate Considerations

Riverside's climate presents specific challenges for HVAC systems:

- **Extreme summer heat** — temperatures regularly exceed 90°F from May through September
- **High humidity** — relative humidity often exceeds 70%, requiring efficient dehumidification
- **Frequent thunderstorms** — power surges and lightning strikes can damage control boards and compressors
- **Year-round operation** — unlike northern climates, Riverside AC systems rarely get a seasonal break
