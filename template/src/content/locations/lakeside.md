### Why Preventive Maintenance Matters

In our region's climate, HVAC systems run hard for eight or more months per year. Without regular maintenance, efficiency degrades, filter systems become restricted, and small mechanical issues compound into expensive failures. Our annual tune-up service addresses all of these potential failure points:

- Cleaning evaporator and condenser coils
- Checking and topping refrigerant levels
- Inspecting electrical connections and contactors
- Flushing condensate drain lines
- Calibrating thermostat and controls
- Testing system capacity and airflow

A maintained system runs cooler, consumes less electricity, and lasts years longer than one that's neglected. Call us to schedule your Lakeside tune-up today.
