We prioritize protecting your private information. This policy describes how we collect and use data when you visit our website or use our services.

## Personal Information Collection

We collect personally identifiable information when you voluntarily provide it, including:

- First and last name
- Mailing address
- Email address
- Phone number

This information may be gathered through account registration, special offers signup, email communication, form submissions, and payment processing.

## Use of Personal Information

We use collected data to operate our website and deliver the services you have requested. We may also use your information to inform you about additional products and services offered by our company and affiliated companies.

## Third-Party Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease customer lists to third parties. We may share data with trusted partners for the purpose of statistical analysis, customer support, communications, and service delivery. All third parties are required to maintain confidentiality and may not use your information for any purpose other than providing these services.

We may disclose your personal information without notice when required to do so by law or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our company, our users, or the public.

## User Behavior Tracking

We may track website visits to understand which services are most popular, and to deliver customized content and advertising based on user interests.

## Automatically Collected Information

Technical data such as IP addresses, browser types, domain names, access times, and referring websites may be automatically collected and used for service operation and usage statistics.

## Cookies

Cookies are used to personalize your online experience and save your preferences. Cookies cannot execute programs or deliver viruses to your computer. You can typically modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.

## Security Measures

Your personal information is protected using SSL Protocol encryption for sensitive data transmission. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of Internet transmission is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## Children Under Thirteen

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of thirteen. If you are under thirteen, you must ask your parent or guardian for permission before using this website.

## Email Communications

From time to time, we may send you emails regarding announcements, promotions, alerts, or surveys. You may opt out of these communications at any time by replying "STOP" or clicking the unsubscribe link provided in the email.

## External Data Storage

Customer data may be stored on servers operated by third-party hosting vendors.

## Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our website.
