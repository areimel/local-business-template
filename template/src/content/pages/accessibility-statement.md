## Our Commitment to Accessibility

We are dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for individuals with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone while applying the relevant accessibility standards.

## Conformance Status

We strive to be partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, meaning that while some content areas may not yet fully meet all accessibility requirements, we are actively working to improve compliance across our website.

Where possible, we have also implemented certain Level AAA criteria, including logo-only text images and enhanced desktop accessibility features.

## Browser & Technology Compatibility

This website relies on HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for its accessibility functionality. It is designed to be compatible with current and recent versions of major web browsers. Browsers older than 3 major versions or mobile operating systems older than 5 years may not be fully supported.

## Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

- Adjustable font sizing capability
- Strong color and contrast ratios for text readability
- Keyboard-accessible navigation throughout the site
- Skip-to-content links at the top of each page
- Semantic HTML structure and proper heading hierarchy
- ARIA labels on interactive elements

## Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of our website may have limitations:

- **Third-party elements:** Plugins and embedded content from external providers may not fully meet WCAG compliance standards.
- **Downloadable documents:** PDFs and other documents created by external parties may not fully meet accessibility standards.
- **Graphics:** Some images may lack text alternatives, particularly decorative graphics. We monitor and address these on an ongoing basis.

## Assistive Technology Support

We recommend the use of assistive technologies for users with visual, hearing, cognitive, or motor impairments. Compatible tools include screen readers, speech recognition software, screen magnifiers, and captioning features built into modern browsers and operating systems.

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website, or if you have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will respond within 3–5 business days.
