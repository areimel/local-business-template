## About the Role

We're seeking a skilled HVAC Service Technician to join our team. You'll be responsible for diagnosing, repairing, and maintaining residential HVAC systems throughout our service area.

## Key Responsibilities

### Diagnosis and Repair
- Conduct thorough diagnostic assessments of HVAC systems to identify issues and recommend appropriate repairs or replacements
- Perform repairs efficiently and accurately to restore proper functioning of air conditioning units

### Maintenance Services
- Perform routine maintenance tasks, including cleaning, lubricating, and adjusting components of HVAC systems to ensure peak performance and efficiency

### Installation Support
- Assist with the installation of new HVAC systems, including handling equipment, running ductwork, and connecting electrical components, under the guidance of senior technicians

### Customer Interaction
- Interact with customers in a professional and courteous manner, providing explanations of services performed and recommendations for optimal HVAC system performance
- Address customer concerns and inquiries promptly and effectively

### Documentation
- Maintain accurate records of services performed, including work orders, service reports, and inventory usage
- Complete paperwork and documentation in a timely and organized manner

### Safety Compliance
- Adhere to all safety protocols and procedures to ensure a safe working environment
- Follow safety guidelines for handling HVAC equipment, tools, and materials

## Requirements

- Must have 1 year of technician experience
- Must have a valid driver's license and clean MVR report
- Must be able to pass a background and drug check
- Must possess the ability to own and use power tools
- Ability to wear company-approved personal protection equipment per local, state, federal, or OSHA regulations
- Must complete tasks within the company's standard timeframe
- This job operates in a construction site environment and may be outside in heat/cold, wet/humid, and dry/arid conditions or inside conditions
