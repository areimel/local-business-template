## Brighten Every Room in Your Home

The right lighting makes a real difference in how you use and enjoy your home. A dim kitchen makes cooking harder. A poorly lit hallway feels unwelcoming. And outdated fixtures waste energy and money every month. Our electricians install recessed lights, pendant fixtures, under-cabinet lighting, dimmer switches, and more — all wired properly and to code.

Whether you are remodeling a single room or upgrading the lighting throughout your entire home, we work with you to choose the right fixtures and placement for your space.

## Outdoor Lighting That Works as Hard as You Do

Local homeowners spend a lot of time outdoors, and the right exterior lighting extends your living space well past sunset. We install landscape lighting to highlight your home's best features, path lighting for safety, and security lighting to deter unwanted visitors. Every outdoor installation is weather-rated and built to handle our region's heat, humidity, and storms.
