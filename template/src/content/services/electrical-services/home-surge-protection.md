## Protect Your Home From Storm Season

Living in our region means living with lightning. The region sees more cloud-to-ground lightning strikes than nearly any other part of the country, and every one of those strikes has the potential to send a destructive power surge into your home. Without proper protection, a single surge can fry your air conditioner's control board, destroy a television or computer, or damage sensitive appliances like your refrigerator and washing machine.

A whole-home surge protector is installed directly at your electrical panel and acts as a gatekeeper. It absorbs excess voltage before it can travel through your home's wiring and reach your devices. Installation takes just a couple of hours, and the investment is a fraction of what it would cost to replace even one major appliance.

## More Than Just Lightning

Power surges do not only come from lightning. They also happen when the power grid fluctuates, when large appliances cycle on and off, and when utility work affects your line. These smaller surges happen dozens of times a day and gradually degrade your electronics over time. A whole-home surge protector handles all of them, keeping your devices running longer and more reliably.
