## Outlets and Switches That Keep Up With Modern Life

Older homes throughout Anytown and the surrounding communities were not wired for the number of devices we rely on today. Overloaded power strips, two-prong outlets without a ground, and switches that spark or feel warm are more than inconveniences — they are safety hazards. We replace outdated electrical devices with modern, code-compliant options that keep your family safe and your home running smoothly.

Our licensed electricians (LICENSE-#00000) install standard outlets, GFCI receptacles, USB charging outlets, dimmer switches, three-way switches, and specialty devices for ceiling fans and appliances. Every installation is grounded, tested, and built to last.

## GFCI and Safety Upgrades

Ground-fault circuit interrupter outlets are designed to cut power instantly when they detect a current imbalance, protecting you from electrical shock in wet or damp locations. If your kitchen, bathroom, garage, or outdoor outlets lack GFCI protection, upgrading is both a code requirement and a critical safety measure. We also install arc-fault circuit interrupter breakers where required to reduce the risk of electrical fires.

## Convenience Upgrades for Every Room

Beyond safety, updated switches and outlets make daily life easier. Dimmer switches let you set the perfect lighting level for any activity. USB outlets eliminate bulky adapters and free up plug space for other devices. Tamper-resistant receptacles protect curious children without changing how adults use the outlet.
