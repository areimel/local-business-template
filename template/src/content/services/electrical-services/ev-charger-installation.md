## Home EV Charging Done Right

More local homeowners are making the switch to electric vehicles every year, and a Level 2 home charger is the most convenient way to keep your vehicle ready to go. Plugging into a standard wall outlet works in a pinch, but it can take 40 hours or more to fully charge a modern EV battery. A dedicated 240-volt Level 2 charger cuts that time down to roughly eight hours, giving you a full charge while you sleep.

## What Goes Into a Professional EV Charger Installation

Installing an EV charger is more than mounting a unit on the wall. It starts with a thorough evaluation of your electrical panel to confirm it has enough capacity and available breaker space for a new high-amperage circuit. From there, we run properly sized wiring from the panel to the charger location, install the correct breaker, and either set up a NEMA 14-50 outlet or hardwire the charger directly depending on the manufacturer's requirements. Every connection is made to code and tested before we hand you the keys to hassle-free home charging.
