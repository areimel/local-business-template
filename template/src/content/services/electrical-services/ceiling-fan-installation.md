## Stay Comfortable and Save Energy

In the local area, your air conditioning system works overtime for most of the year. A ceiling fan is one of the simplest and most cost-effective ways to reduce that load. By circulating air throughout the room, a ceiling fan lets you raise your thermostat a few degrees while still feeling cool and comfortable. In the cooler months, reversing the fan direction pushes warm air back down from the ceiling.

Our electricians install ceiling fans in bedrooms, living rooms, covered patios, kitchens, and anywhere else you need better air circulation. We work with any fan you purchase, or we can help you choose the right model for your room.

## Professional Installation Makes the Difference

A ceiling fan that is not properly mounted can wobble, make noise, or worse — come loose from the ceiling. Every fan we install is secured to a fan-rated electrical box, wired correctly, and balanced before we leave. If your room does not have an existing fan location, we run the wiring and install a proper support box so the fan is safe and stable from day one.
