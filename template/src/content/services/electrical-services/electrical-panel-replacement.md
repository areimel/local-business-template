## When It Is Time to Upgrade Your Electrical Panel

Many homes in Anytown and the surrounding area were built with 100-amp panels that simply cannot support the electrical demands of modern living. Adding a new HVAC system, hot tub, home office equipment, or EV charger can push an older panel past its safe limits. Panels from recalled manufacturers like Federal Pacific and Zinsco pose an even greater risk because their breakers may fail to trip during an overload, creating a serious fire hazard.

We have been serving local homeowners since 1990. Our licensed electricians (LICENSE-#00000) evaluate your entire electrical system before recommending an upgrade, so you get exactly the capacity you need without paying for work you do not.

## What to Expect During a Panel Upgrade

We start by disconnecting the existing panel and coordinating any necessary utility work. The new panel is mounted, circuits are transferred and labeled, and all connections are torqued to manufacturer specifications. Once installation is complete, we test every breaker and verify grounding and bonding before restoring power. We also schedule the required municipal inspection on your behalf, giving you confidence that the work meets every applicable code.

## Protecting Your Home Investment

A modern electrical panel does more than prevent tripped breakers. It increases the resale value of your home, supports future renovations without costly rewiring, and reduces the risk of electrical fires. Whether you need a straightforward breaker replacement or a full 200-amp service upgrade, we deliver safe, lasting results backed by decades of local experience.
