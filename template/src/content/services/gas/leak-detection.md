## Why Gas Leaks Require Immediate Attention

Natural gas and propane are odorized with a sulfur-like scent specifically so leaks can be detected by smell. However, not all leaks produce a strong enough odor to notice right away, especially small leaks in outdoor lines or poorly ventilated spaces. Left unaddressed, even a minor leak can lead to dangerous gas accumulation, health risks from prolonged exposure, and in worst-case scenarios, fire or explosion. That is why professional detection using calibrated equipment is essential whenever a leak is suspected.

## Protecting Your Family With Professional Detection

Our technicians follow a systematic approach to every gas leak call. We start by assessing the immediate safety of the area, then use electronic gas detectors to sweep all gas lines, connections, and appliances. If a leak is found, we isolate the affected section, complete the repair, and run a full pressure test to confirm the system is sealed. We also check nearby connections to make sure the issue has not affected other parts of your gas system. Every step is documented so you have a clear record of the work performed.
