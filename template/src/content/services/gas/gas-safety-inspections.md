## What We Check During a Gas Safety Inspection

A thorough gas safety inspection goes well beyond a visual check. We start at the gas meter and work through every supply line, branch line, and connection in your home. Each gas appliance is inspected for proper operation, correct venting, and safe clearances. We test gas pressure at multiple points to confirm the system is delivering the right amount of fuel. Shutoff valves are tested to make sure they function correctly in an emergency. Carbon monoxide readings are taken near every combustion appliance and in adjacent living spaces.

## Why Annual Inspections Matter in the local area

Heat and humidity in our region create conditions that accelerate corrosion on metal fittings and gas connectors, particularly in areas with limited airflow like utility closets and attics. Insects and rodents can also damage gas line insulation and connections. An annual inspection catches these issues before they compromise your safety. If you have not had your gas system inspected in more than a year, or if you have recently moved into a home with gas appliances, scheduling an inspection is a straightforward way to confirm everything is operating safely.
