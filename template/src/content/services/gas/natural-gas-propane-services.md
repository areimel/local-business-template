## Gas Line Installation and Appliance Hookups

Running a new gas line or connecting a gas appliance requires precise measurements, proper materials, and code-compliant installation. We handle gas line installations for kitchen remodels, new construction additions, outdoor living areas, and appliance upgrades. Every run is pressure tested and inspected before we consider the job complete. If your home currently uses electric appliances and you want to switch to gas, we can plan the entire conversion from supply line to final connection.

## Propane System Service for Local Homes

Many homes in Anytown and the surrounding area rely on propane for water heating, cooking, and backup heating. Our technicians service propane regulators, supply lines, and appliance connections to keep your system running safely and efficiently. We also help homeowners evaluate whether natural gas or propane is the better fit for their property, taking into account availability, cost, and long-term performance.
