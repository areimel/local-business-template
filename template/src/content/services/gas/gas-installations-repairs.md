## Gas Line Installation for New Construction and Remodels

Adding gas service to a new room, connecting an outdoor kitchen, or running a gas line during a remodel requires careful planning and precise execution. We work with homeowners and contractors to determine the best routing, pipe sizing, and connection points for each project. All installations use code-approved materials rated for the specific application, and every new line is pressure tested at above operating pressure to confirm integrity before gas is introduced.

## Gas Appliance Repair and Pipe Repair

When a gas appliance stops working correctly or a gas pipe develops a problem, a prompt repair is important for both safety and convenience. We diagnose and repair gas water heaters, furnaces, stoves, dryers, and other gas-powered equipment. For pipe issues, we assess the condition of the affected section, determine whether a spot repair or a longer replacement is needed, and complete the work with proper fittings and connections. Every repair includes a leak test and operational check before we leave your home.
