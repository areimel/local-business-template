## Catching Leaks Before They Become Costly

Water leaks are one of the most damaging and expensive plumbing problems a homeowner can face. In the local area, slab leaks are especially common due to shifting soil and the limestone foundation conditions in Anytowns. A small pinhole leak under your slab can go undetected for weeks, leading to foundation damage, mold growth, and sky-high water bills.

Our team is licensed (LICENSE-#00000) and trained to detect leaks in every part of your plumbing system — supply lines, drain lines, slab pipes, and outdoor water lines. We do not guess. We use the right equipment to confirm the location before any repair work begins.

## When to Call for Leak Detection

If your water bill has spiked without explanation, you notice warm or damp spots on your floor, or you hear water running when nothing is turned on, do not wait. The longer a leak goes unaddressed, the more damage it causes. Call us and we will get a technician to your home quickly to find the source and stop the problem before it gets worse.
