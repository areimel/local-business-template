## Common Causes of Clogged Drains

Drain clogs are one of the most common plumbing issues homeowners face in the local area. Kitchen drains often clog from grease, food particles, and soap buildup. Bathroom drains collect hair, soap scum, and toothpaste residue over time. Outdoor and sewer line drains can be affected by tree root intrusion, especially in older neighborhoods around Anytown where mature landscaping is common. Understanding what causes the clog helps us choose the right solution and prevent it from happening again.

## Our Drain Cleaning Methods

We bring professional-grade tools to every job. For standard household clogs, a motorized drain snake can break through and remove the blockage quickly. For stubborn or recurring problems, we use hydro-jetting — a high-pressure water system that scours the interior walls of your pipes and removes buildup that snaking alone cannot reach. If we suspect a deeper issue such as a collapsed pipe or root intrusion, we can perform a camera inspection to see exactly what is happening inside the line.

## Keeping Your Drains Clear

Prevention goes a long way toward avoiding plumbing headaches. We recommend using drain screens in showers and sinks, avoiding pouring grease or oil down kitchen drains, and scheduling periodic professional drain cleaning to remove buildup before it becomes a blockage. Our team is happy to walk you through simple habits that will keep your drains flowing freely year-round.
