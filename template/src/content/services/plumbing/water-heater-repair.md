## Common Water Heater Problems We Fix

Water heaters give plenty of warning signs before they fail completely. If you are noticing lukewarm water, fluctuating temperatures, discolored or rusty water, unusual noises, or visible leaks around the base of the unit, something is wrong. Common causes include failed heating elements, a tripped reset button, a worn-out thermostat, a depleted anode rod, or sediment buildup in the tank.

Our technicians are trained to troubleshoot all of these issues across every major brand and fuel type. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks so many repairs can be completed in a single visit.

## Honest Advice When You Need It

We understand that a water heater repair is an unplanned expense, and we will never push a replacement when a repair makes sense. But we will also be straight with you when a unit is at the end of its life and putting more money into it is not worth it. Our goal is to give you the information you need to make the best decision for your home and budget.
