## How Tankless Water Heaters Work

Unlike traditional tank water heaters that keep 40 to 80 gallons of water heated around the clock, tankless systems only heat water when you turn on a faucet or appliance. Cold water flows through a heat exchanger powered by gas or electricity, and hot water is delivered within seconds. When you turn the fixture off, the unit stops heating — which eliminates the standby energy loss that makes tank water heaters less efficient.

This on-demand approach is especially appealing in our region, where the incoming water temperature is relatively warm year-round. That means tankless units do not have to work as hard to reach your desired temperature, which translates to even greater efficiency gains compared to colder climates.

## Is Tankless Right for Your Home?

Tankless water heaters are a great fit for many homes, but they are not the best choice for every situation. Homes with very high simultaneous hot water demand may need multiple units or a high-capacity model. Switching from a tank to tankless may also require electrical upgrades or gas line modifications. We evaluate all of these factors during our in-home assessment so you know exactly what is involved before making a decision.
