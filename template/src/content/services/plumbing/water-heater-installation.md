## Getting the Installation Right the First Time

An undersized water heater leaves your family running out of hot water. An oversized one wastes energy every month. And a poorly installed unit can lead to leaks, code violations, or premature failure. That is why proper sizing and professional installation matter so much.

Our team takes the time to understand your household's hot water needs before recommending a system. We factor in the number of people in your home, how many bathrooms you have, and whether you run multiple hot water appliances at the same time.

## Built for Local Conditions

Hard water in our region creates unique challenges for water heaters. Mineral buildup accelerates wear on heating elements and reduces efficiency over time. When we install your new water heater, we make sure it is set up to handle local water conditions — and we will let you know if a water treatment system would help extend its lifespan.
