## When Is It Time to Repipe Your Home

Many homes in the local area were built with galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that degrade over time. Galvanized pipes corrode from the inside out, restricting water flow and introducing rust into your water supply. Polybutylene pipes, common in homes built between the 1970s and 1990s, are prone to cracking and sudden failure. If you are dealing with recurring leaks, discolored water, or noticeably low pressure throughout your home, a full repipe may be the most reliable long-term solution.

## Benefits of Modern Piping

Replacing aging pipes with copper or PEX provides consistent water pressure, cleaner water, and protection against costly leaks and water damage. Modern piping materials are resistant to corrosion and scale buildup, meaning your plumbing system will perform reliably for decades. A repipe also adds value to your home and can give you peace of mind during our region's demanding summer months when water usage is at its highest.
