## Why Hybrid Water Heaters Make Sense in Our Region

Hybrid heat pump water heaters are designed to pull heat from the air around them, which means they perform best in warm climates. Our region's year-round warm temperatures make it one of the best regions in the country for this technology. A hybrid unit installed in a garage here operates near peak efficiency for most of the year, delivering the maximum energy savings the technology is capable of.

The added benefit is that hybrid units also dehumidify and slightly cool the space around them — a welcome side effect in a hot, humid garage or utility room in our region.

## Understanding the Investment

Hybrid water heaters have a higher purchase price than standard electric tank units, but the energy savings are substantial. Most homeowners see a return on the additional investment within three to five years through lower electric bills. Factor in available utility rebates and federal tax credits, and the effective cost difference narrows considerably. We walk every customer through the numbers so you can see exactly how the investment pays off over time.
