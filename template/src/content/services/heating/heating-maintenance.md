## Why Annual Heating Maintenance Matters

In our region, your heating system may only run for a few weeks each year — but that doesn't mean it can be ignored. Systems that sit unused for long periods can develop dust buildup on burners, corroded electrical connections, and worn safety components. When a cold front arrives and you turn on the heat for the first time, these hidden issues can lead to breakdowns, poor performance, or even safety hazards.

Annual maintenance catches these problems early and keeps your system running safely and efficiently when you need it most.

## What to Expect During a Maintenance Visit

Our technicians perform a thorough inspection of every critical component in your heating system. This includes checking and tightening electrical connections, inspecting the heat exchanger for cracks or corrosion, testing safety controls, cleaning the blower motor and burner assembly, replacing the air filter, and calibrating your thermostat. After the visit, we'll provide a clear report on your system's condition and let you know if any components need attention in the near future.
