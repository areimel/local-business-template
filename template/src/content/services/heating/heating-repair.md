## Common Reasons a Heater Stops Working

There are several reasons your heater may stop producing warm air. Sometimes a system has simply reached the end of its useful life, but in many cases, the fix is straightforward. Common causes include faulty thermostats, tripped breakers, clogged air filters, worn ignitors, and failing blower motors.

Our technicians are trained to identify the root cause and provide honest recommendations — whether that's a simple repair or a system upgrade.

## Signs You May Need Heating Repair

If your heater runs but doesn't warm your home, cycles on and off frequently, produces unusual noises or burning smells, or if your thermostat settings don't match the room temperature, it's time to schedule a repair. Catching these issues early can prevent more costly breakdowns and keep your system running safely through the cooler months.
