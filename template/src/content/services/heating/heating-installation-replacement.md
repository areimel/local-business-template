## When to Replace Your Heating System

If your heating system is breaking down frequently, heats your home unevenly, or is driving up your energy costs, it may be time for a replacement. In our region's climate, where systems run roughly twice the annual hours compared to the national average, major wear can begin around 7-8 years. Modern heating systems are significantly more energy-efficient than older models, which means lower utility bills and more consistent comfort throughout your home.

Our team will evaluate your current system, explain the pros and cons of repair versus replacement, and help you make an informed decision.

## Choosing the Right System for Your Home

Not all heating systems are created equal. The right system for your home depends on factors like square footage, insulation levels, ductwork condition, and your budget. We take the time to understand your needs and recommend a system that delivers reliable comfort without wasting energy. Whether you need a heat pump, electric furnace, or gas furnace, we'll guide you through the options.
