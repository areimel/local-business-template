## How Heat Pumps Work

Unlike traditional furnaces that generate heat by burning fuel or using electric resistance, heat pumps work by transferring heat from one place to another. In heating mode, they extract warmth from the outdoor air and move it inside your home. In cooling mode, they reverse the process. This heat-transfer approach uses significantly less energy than heat generation, which is why heat pumps are one of the most efficient options for local homeowners.

## Common Heat Pump Issues

Heat pumps can experience problems that are unique to their dual-function design. Common issues include the system getting stuck in cooling mode, blowing cool air when set to heat, poor performance during cold mornings, frequent cycling, and ice buildup on the outdoor unit. If you notice any of these symptoms, scheduling a service call early can prevent more costly damage and keep your system running efficiently through the season.
