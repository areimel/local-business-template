## Signs You May Need a Dehumidifier

Our region's climate means high humidity is a near-constant challenge for homeowners. If your home feels humid and sticky even with the AC running, windows fog up regularly, you notice musty smells in closets or bathrooms, or the air just feels clammy, a whole-home dehumidifier may be the solution.

Unlike portable dehumidifiers that only treat one room at a time, a whole-home system integrates directly with your HVAC ductwork to control moisture levels throughout your entire house automatically.

## How Dehumidification Improves Comfort

Excess humidity doesn't just make your home feel uncomfortable — it also forces your AC to work harder. When your air conditioner has to handle both cooling and moisture removal, it runs longer and less efficiently. A dedicated dehumidifier takes the moisture burden off your AC, allowing it to focus on what it does best: cooling your home. The result is better comfort, lower energy bills, and less wear on your air conditioning system.
