## Common Indoor Air Quality Issues

Many homeowners in our area deal with indoor air that feels dusty, heavy, or stale without realizing that their HVAC system plays a central role. Common issues include musty odors, excessive dust buildup, worsening allergy symptoms, stale or stuffy air, and humidity-related discomfort. These problems are often made worse by high humidity in our region, which can promote mold growth, increase dust mite activity, and make indoor spaces feel uncomfortable even when the temperature is right.

## Solutions That Make a Difference

Improving indoor air quality often involves a combination of approaches. Upgraded filtration captures more airborne particles before they circulate through your home. UV air purifiers neutralize biological contaminants like mold and bacteria. Proper ventilation ensures stale air is replaced with fresh, filtered air. And whole-home dehumidification keeps moisture levels in check, addressing one of the most common root causes of poor air quality in homes in our area. We help you identify which solutions will have the biggest impact for your specific situation.
