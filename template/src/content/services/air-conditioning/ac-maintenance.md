## What AC Maintenance Includes

A typical maintenance visit includes checking system performance, testing electrical components, inspecting airflow and temperatures, cleaning components as needed, and spotting worn parts early. These steps help ensure your system is ready to handle our region's demanding cooling season.

Maintenance is especially important before the hottest months, when your AC will be working its hardest. Catching a small issue in spring can prevent a major breakdown in July.

## Benefits of Routine Maintenance

Homeowners who keep up with regular maintenance typically experience fewer emergency repair calls, lower energy costs, better humidity control, and a longer overall system lifespan. In a climate where your AC runs nearly year-round, skipping maintenance can lead to problems that are far more costly than the tune-up itself.
