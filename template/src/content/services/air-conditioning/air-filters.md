## Signs Your Filter May Be Causing Problems

If your AC is running constantly or airflow feels weak, checking the filter is often the first step. Common signs of a filter-related issue include weak airflow from vents, more dust buildup around the house, worsening allergy symptoms, an AC that feels less effective than normal, and higher-than-expected power bills.

A clogged or low-quality filter forces your system to work harder, which not only reduces comfort but also increases energy consumption and accelerates wear on critical components.

## Choosing the Right Filter

Not all filters are created equal. Filters are rated using the MERV scale, which measures how effectively they capture particles. Higher-rated filters trap smaller particles, but they also need to be compatible with your system to maintain proper airflow. Our team can help you find the right balance between filtration quality and system performance, so you get cleaner air without putting unnecessary strain on your equipment.
