## When Your Thermostat Is the Problem

Many homeowners assume their AC is failing when the real issue is the thermostat. Inaccurate temperature readings, a blank display, or a system that won't turn on can all point to a thermostat malfunction rather than a compressor or refrigerant problem. We troubleshoot thermostat issues and install replacements, including smart thermostat options that offer more precise control.

Our thermostat services include replacement, smart thermostat installation, wiring troubleshooting, and calibration and setup. We make sure your thermostat communicates properly with your system so you get consistent, reliable comfort.

## The Benefits of Upgrading

Upgrading from an older manual or basic programmable thermostat to a modern smart thermostat can improve both comfort and efficiency. Smart thermostats learn your schedule, adjust temperatures automatically when you're away, and let you control your system from your phone. For local homeowners running their AC most of the year, even small efficiency gains from better temperature control can add up to meaningful savings.
