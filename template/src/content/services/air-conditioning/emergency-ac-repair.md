## When Your AC Fails, Every Minute Counts

When air conditioning stops working during extreme heat, it becomes urgent quickly. Rising indoor temperatures can be uncomfortable at best and dangerous at worst, especially for young children, elderly family members, and pets. Emergency AC repair addresses situations where home temperature is climbing rapidly or the system shows signs of serious failure.

You may need emergency service if your AC won't turn on, it's blowing warm air and getting worse, you smell burning or electrical odors, or breakers keep tripping. These are signs that something is seriously wrong and waiting could make the problem worse.
